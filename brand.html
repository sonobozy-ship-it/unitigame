03 — App Identity

Icon Design Principles

The icon distills the entire game into one symbol: a glowing koru spiral drawn like a pipe on a grid. At every size, the shape reads as both a natural form and a puzzle path.

  • Background Deep Void #0A0A14 with subtle gradient
  • Spiral Jade Flow gradient #2A9B84 → #7EEACC
  • Grid dots Soft Pearl at 6% opacity
  • Stroke 8px rounded linecap
  • Corner 28px radius (iOS/Android safe)
  • Effect Glow filter on spiral path
